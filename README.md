# [farmer-online]
  CAPSTONE PROJECT 
  
  The demand for clean, safe food is increasingly rising among consumers. When purchasing agricultural products from supermarkets, there can be concerns about product quality and unclear origins. Additionally, the     increasing prices of these products make it difficult for consumers to afford them.
  
  Our platform offers a completely new experience, allowing everyone to enjoy farming without the constraints of time and space. In agriculture, raising large numbers of livestock and poultry requires high costs,   carries significant risks, and often involves substantial investments. Farmers can also be pressured by middlemen, leading them to use growth-promoting drugs that are not beneficial for the animals. Our platform serves as an intermediary, ensuring that farmers can focus more on quality without worrying about being undercut.
  
With our platform, farmers can be assured of fair prices and peace of mind, knowing they can sell their products at higher prices. Consumers, on the other hand, will have the confidence of knowing the origin of the animals they purchase, ensuring a trustworthy and transparent supply chain.
